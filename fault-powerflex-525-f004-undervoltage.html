PowerFlex 525 F004 Undervoltage — What to Check

F004 indicates that the drive DC bus fell below its minimum threshold. A drop or interruption in the incoming supply is a key possibility.

Updated 27 October 2026 · Manufacturer source linked below
Quick answer: F004 — Undervoltage

F004 indicates that the drive DC bus fell below its minimum threshold. A drop or interruption in the incoming supply is a key possibility.

Symptoms to look for

  • Fault appears after a short supply dip or interruption.
  • Several drives trip around the same time.
  • The event coincides with other equipment switching on.

Step-by-step diagnostic checklist

  1. Note whether other equipment or drives recorded a disturbance at the same time.
  2. Check the drive's rated input voltage against the installation documentation and supply specification.
  3. Arrange qualified checks for low incoming voltage, loose or interrupted supply connections and upstream protection according to safe electrical procedures.
  4. Use the fault history to separate a one-off supply event from repeated undervoltage. Investigate the cause before enabling automatic restarts.

Record readings and event history before clearing a fault. Use only checks permitted by your site's risk assessment and equipment manual.

Important precautions

Do not infer that an F004 alone proves a defective drive. Live mains and DC-bus testing must be performed only by competent personnel using an approved safe system of work.
