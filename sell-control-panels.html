How panels are assessed

STEP 01

Send clear photographs

Photograph the panel exterior, the open cabinet and visible part numbers. A machine description and whether it was removed working are also useful.

STEP 02

Component review

We identify the equipment with realistic resale demand, along with low-value, obsolete or difficult-to-test components that may affect the offer.

STEP 03

Choose the practical route

Depending on value and logistics, we may offer for the whole panel, selected components, a managed resale arrangement or collection as part of a larger clearance.

What usually creates value

Modern, obsolete or hard-to-source automation equipment can make a panel commercially interesting even when the enclosure and standard switchgear have limited resale value.

✓PLCs and processor modules
✓HMIs and operator panels
✓Variable-speed and servo drives
✓Safety PLCs and specialist safety I/O
✓Remote I/O and communication modules
✓Industrial PCs and specialist controllers
IMPORTANT

A panel quote is not simply the total of the highest online asking prices

The commercial offer must account for removal, testing, missing connectors, condition, storage, individual selling costs, slow-moving components and warranty exposure. When outright purchase is not the best fit, selective purchase or managed resale may preserve more value.
