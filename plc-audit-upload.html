Confidentiality & retention

How the project is handled

Uploads are private and use opaque storage paths. Permanent Blob credentials remain server-side. The signed upload permission expires quickly and is scoped to the selected job.

The audit record stores the original filename for your dashboard, but the object pathname does not use your project name. Retention is recorded on each AuditJob and the customer can request deletion from the audit page.

The analysis result describes the supplied project. It is not proof of live machine behaviour or a substitute for online checks, safety validation or commissioning.

For customers that cannot upload source code, a future/local Windows analysis option is planned.
