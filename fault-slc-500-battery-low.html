SLC 500 Battery Low — Protect the PLC Program

On applicable SLC 500 processors, the BATTERY LOW indicator means RAM backup support is at risk. Preserve the program and confirm the processor-specific replacement procedure before power-off.

Updated 27 October 2026 · Manufacturer source linked below
Quick answer: BATTERY LOW — Memory backup warning

On applicable SLC 500 processors, the BATTERY LOW indicator means RAM backup support is at risk. Preserve the program and confirm the processor-specific replacement procedure before power-off.

Symptoms to look for

  • BATTERY LOW LED is lit on an SLC processor.
  • The age of the lithium battery is unknown.
  • No current offline PLC backup is readily available.

Step-by-step diagnostic checklist

  1. Read the complete processor catalog number: battery types and replacement procedures differ by model.
  2. Before switching off a system with low battery, verify that a retrievable, current project backup exists and that the site has the means to restore it.
  3. Refer to the exact Rockwell SLC 500 processor procedure and site safety assessment for battery service. Some manuals specify replacement with processor power applied; this must not be treated as a general instruction for all processors.
  4. Document battery replacement date and separately verify retention/restore arrangements under a controlled maintenance plan.

Record readings and event history before clearing a fault. Use only checks permitted by your site's risk assessment and equipment manual.

Important precautions

Never assume turning off the PLC is safe after a BATTERY LOW warning. Do not attempt powered battery replacement without a documented model-specific method, competent authorisation and machine-risk controls.
