PowerFlex 525 F005 Overvoltage — Fault Guide

F005 means the DC bus voltage exceeded its limit. Supply transients or motor regeneration during deceleration are possible causes.

Updated 27 October 2026 · Manufacturer source linked below
Quick answer: F005 — Overvoltage

F005 means the DC bus voltage exceeded its limit. Supply transients or motor regeneration during deceleration are possible causes.

Symptoms to look for

  • Fault occurs during braking or deceleration.
  • Fault appears during a supply disturbance.
  • A rapid speed change precedes the fault.

Step-by-step diagnostic checklist

  1. Record the drive's exact model, firmware and the operating stage when F005 occurred.
  2. For a trip during deceleration, review commanded ramp times, load inertia and the permitted braking strategy for this drive.
  3. For a trip outside deceleration, consider supply overvoltage or transients and arrange suitable input supply checks.
  4. Only change configuration or braking hardware after checking Rockwell's manual and the machine's safe stopping requirements.

Record readings and event history before clearing a fault. Use only checks permitted by your site's risk assessment and equipment manual.

Important precautions

Do not increase deceleration time where doing so would compromise a safety function or required stopping performance. Never short, probe or touch exposed DC bus connections without appropriate authorised procedures.
