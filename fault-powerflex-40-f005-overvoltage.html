PowerFlex 40 F005 (F5) Overvoltage Fault — Causes & Checks

The DC bus voltage rose above the drive's allowed limit. Check whether the trip happens on deceleration or alongside supply disturbances.

Updated 27 October 2026 · Manufacturer source linked below
Quick answer: F005 / F5 — Overvoltage

The DC bus voltage rose above the drive's allowed limit. Check whether the trip happens on deceleration or alongside supply disturbances.

Symptoms to look for

  • Fault appears while slowing a high-inertia load.
  • The fault follows a mains surge or transient.
  • Drive trips again after a reset because the underlying condition remains.

Step-by-step diagnostic checklist

  1. Record the full drive catalog number, fault history and whether the trip occurs on acceleration, at constant speed or during deceleration.
  2. If the fault occurs on deceleration, review whether the motor is regenerating into the DC bus. Check the programmed deceleration time against the application and mechanical stop requirements.
  3. If it occurs without deceleration, investigate documented supply-quality events and input overvoltage with a qualified electrician.
  4. Review the manufacturer's permitted braking options for the particular drive and motor before considering any change. Verify stop behaviour and risk assessment after changes.

Record readings and event history before clearing a fault. Use only checks permitted by your site's risk assessment and equipment manual.

Important precautions

Do not disable fault protection or repeatedly auto-restart a drive into an unresolved fault. DC-link capacitors remain hazardous after power is removed; follow the manufacturer's isolation and discharge instructions.
