Platforms we source

The kit that keeps UK factories running long after the brochure disappeared: Siemens S5 and S7-300/400 families, Allen-Bradley SLC 500 and PLC-5, older PanelViews and SIMATIC HMIs, discontinued PowerFlex and Micromaster drives, Omron C-series and CJ1, Mitsubishi A-series and FX1/FX2 — and the awkward one-off modules, comms cards and power supplies that stop a whole rack when they fail.

Because we also buy surplus stock and clear factories, obsolete parts flow through our hands constantly. If we don't have your part on the shelf, there's a good chance we know who does — or which decommissioned line it's sitting in.

A word of honest advice from the factory floor: if your machine depends on a part that's already obsolete, the cheapest spare you'll ever buy is the one you buy before it fails. Ask us about building a small critical-spares holding — it's a fraction of the cost of one emergency breakdown.

Common questions

How fast can you find a part?

Sometimes same day if it's in our stock; typically days for network sourcing. Genuine unobtainium we'll tell you straight rather than string you along.

Are sourced parts guaranteed?

Everything we can power up gets bench-tested before dispatch, and we describe condition honestly — you'll know exactly what you're buying.

Can you repair instead of replace?

Sometimes — and where a repair or a compatible substitute makes more sense than a hunt, we'll say so.

Do you buy obsolete stock too?

Constantly — it's half our trade. If you're holding legacy spares you'll never use, we pay cash for them.
