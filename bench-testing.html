What a basic bench test can include

The exact checks depend on the equipment family, available fixtures and the scope agreed before work starts.

Visual & safe power-up

Condition inspection, obvious damage checks and controlled energisation where the unit can be powered safely on the bench.

Diagnostics & recognition

Status LEDs, hardware diagnostics and online recognition where suitable software, interfaces and test hardware are available.

Basic functional checks

Equipment-family checks such as accessible I/O, power-supply output/status or operating-mode checks where practical and agreed.

Full machine simulation, advanced communications/network proving, component-level repair diagnosis and deliberate alteration of customer programs or parameters are outside the basic scope unless separately agreed.
