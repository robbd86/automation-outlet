PowerFlex 525 F013 Ground Fault — Meaning & Checks

The drive has detected a current path to earth from at least one output terminal.

What this fault indicates

The drive has detected a current path to earth from at least one output terminal.

The code alone does not prove that a drive has failed. Keep a record of its complete model number, fault history and what the machine was doing before any reset.

Diagnostic checklist

  1. The code alone does not prove that a drive has failed. Keep a record of its complete model number, fault history and what the machine was doing before any reset.
  2. Record whether the event happened following motor or cable maintenance, moisture exposure or a trip under load.
  3. Arrange isolation and competent inspection of the motor cable, terminations and motor for insulation faults.
  4. Do not reconnect repeatedly to test a suspected earth fault; identify and correct the defect first.

Safety before inspection

Only trained personnel should inspect industrial drives. Isolate and secure the machine before mechanical or electrical work, follow the manufacturer's DC-bus discharge time and verification procedure, and never bypass safety circuits or repeatedly auto-restart a drive with an unresolved fault.
