A few things worth knowing

Frequently asked questions

Can other companies see our inventory or factory details?

No. This is a confidential brokered model, not an open marketplace for factory inventory. AO would discuss any proposed sharing of specific details with you before approaching a potential counterparty.

Do we need a stock list to join?

No. Registration only asks for basic contact details and what type of opportunity interests you. A list or part number can come later where appropriate.

Is the pilot already operating at scale?

No. We are inviting founding factories and assessing interest. Registration does not guarantee a match, supply or entry into the pilot.

Does registering oblige us to sell, purchase or share anything?

No. Registering is non-binding. Any individual sale, sourcing request or consignment arrangement would be discussed and agreed separately.

Is this only for Siemens equipment?

No. The idea is broader industrial automation spares, including Allen-Bradley, Mitsubishi, Omron and other manufacturers. Older and obsolete items are especially relevant.

What if we only need one urgent replacement?

You can use our obsolete parts sourcing service immediately. Pilot registration is for an ongoing connection rather than a guaranteed emergency procurement channel.
