S7-300 BF / BUSF LED — PROFIBUS Troubleshooting

Quick answer: BF / BUSF — PROFIBUS bus fault

On an S7-300 PROFIBUS interface, BF/BUSF points to a bus or configured-station communication problem. The exact meaning depends on the CPU/interface and whether the LED is on or flashing.

Symptoms to look for

  • A BF/BUSF LED is steady or flashing.
  • Configured remote I/O or a DP slave may be unavailable.
  • Communications fail after a connector, cable, station or address change.

Step-by-step diagnostic checklist

  1. Identify the exact S7-300 CPU/interface and whether BF/BUSF is steady or flashing. Different variants use different LEDs.
  2. Read the CPU diagnostic buffer and the DP station diagnostics in STEP 7 to identify which station is missing or reporting an error.
  3. Under safe isolated conditions, inspect the relevant PROFIBUS connectors, cable continuity/condition, termination arrangement and station power.
  4. Check station addresses, the configured network and any recent changes. Restore service only after reviewing the consequences of missing I/O.

Record readings and event history before clearing a fault. Use only checks permitted by your site's risk assessment and equipment manual.

Important precautions

Never unplug live bus wiring or alter termination indiscriminately on a running process. A bus fault can place remote outputs in their configured fault states and may affect machine safety.
