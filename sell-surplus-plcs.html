Three ways to sell

The best route depends on whether speed, certainty or maximum potential return matters most.

CASH

Immediate purchase

A straightforward trade-price offer. We arrange collection and take on testing, storage, selling fees, warranty and return risk.

SELECTIVE

Strongest items only

We buy the fastest-moving PLCs and modules outright, allowing you to retain lower-demand stock rather than discounting the whole lot.

MANAGED

Managed resale

Potentially a higher return for valuable or specialist stock. We test, photograph, market and manage buyers, with payment as items sell.
