Questions the audit answers

BACKUP

Do you have a usable project?

Check whether PLC, HMI and configuration backups exist, whether their source/version is understood and whether critical files are missing.

ACCESS

Can you actually get online?

Record software requirements, versions, licences, communications hardware, firmware and known access constraints.

REPLACE

What if hardware has failed?

Identify whether compatible replacement hardware is realistic, whether a spare is held and where an obsolete component creates recovery risk.
