Siemens S7-300 SF Red LED — Diagnostic Guide

Quick answer: SF LED — System fault

An illuminated SF (system fault) LED indicates that the CPU or connected equipment has recorded a hardware, software or configuration-related fault. The diagnostic buffer is the starting point.

Symptoms to look for

  • Red SF LED is lit on the CPU or a module.
  • CPU may be in RUN or STOP depending on the fault.
  • A new fault appeared after I/O, communications or program changes.

Step-by-step diagnostic checklist

  1. Record the CPU order number and exact LEDs (SF, RUN, STOP, BF, BATF if fitted) before resetting anything.
  2. Connect with a suitable authorised STEP 7 engineering station and read the CPU diagnostic buffer. Preserve the first fault event and its timestamp.
  3. Use diagnostic event details to identify whether the issue is a module, missing I/O, programming exception, supply or parameter issue.
  4. Inspect the specific affected module or subsystem under an approved safe procedure; confirm resolution in the buffer, rather than simply extinguishing the LED.

Record readings and event history before clearing a fault. Use only checks permitted by your site's risk assessment and equipment manual.

Important precautions

An SF LED by itself does not identify a failed CPU. Do not perform memory reset, force I/O or download a replacement program just to clear the lamp; these actions can erase data or create hazardous movement.
