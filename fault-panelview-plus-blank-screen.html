PanelView Plus Blank Screen — Diagnose Power, Display or Runtime

A black PanelView Plus screen does not always mean a failed display. The power supply, backlight, terminal startup and FactoryTalk View ME runtime need to be distinguished.

Updated 27 October 2026 · Manufacturer source linked below
Quick answer: Blank screen — Display / startup issue

A black PanelView Plus screen does not always mean a failed display. The power supply, backlight, terminal startup and FactoryTalk View ME runtime need to be distinguished.

Symptoms to look for

  • Screen is blank but the terminal appears to be powered.
  • Splash/startup screens appear but the application never runs.
  • The display is visible yet the configured project does not start.

Step-by-step diagnostic checklist

  1. Note the exact 2711P terminal catalog number and whether status indicators, sounds or backlight are present.
  2. Determine whether the problem is a completely dark terminal, a dark display on an apparently running terminal, or an application that fails after boot.
  3. Where the display operates, check configuration mode and whether the intended FactoryTalk View ME runtime (.MER) and startup screen are compatible and configured.
  4. Before replacing hardware, establish whether backups of the application and communication configuration are available; record firmware and hardware revision.

Record readings and event history before clearing a fault. Use only checks permitted by your site's risk assessment and equipment manual.

Important precautions

Do not factory-reset or reload firmware without first assessing the available application backup and configuration. Do not open powered HMI enclosures or bypass panel safety interlocks.
