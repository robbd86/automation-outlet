Choose the right selling route

There is no single best way to sell every lot. We will recommend the route that best matches your priorities, the equipment and the likely resale timescale.

ROUTE 01

Immediate cash purchase

The fastest and simplest option. We make a trade-price offer, arrange collection and take on the testing, storage, selling fees, warranty and return risk.

ROUTE 02

Selective purchase

We buy the strongest and fastest-moving items outright. You keep the lower-demand equipment rather than accepting one heavily discounted offer for the entire lot.

ROUTE 03

Managed resale

Potentially a higher overall return for valuable or specialist equipment. We test, photograph, advertise and manage buyers, with payment made as items sell.

HOW PRICING WORKS

Why a cash offer is lower than an online asking price

Outright purchase offers are trade prices, not end-user retail valuations. Advertised prices are not always achieved prices, and industrial parts can take months to sell. Our offer accounts for realistic demand, condition, testing, collection, marketplace fees, storage, warranty exposure and the risk that an item is returned or never sells.

If immediate payment is not your priority, managed resale may provide a better route to a higher return.

How it works

STEP 01

Send photos or a list

A few phone photos or a parts list is enough to start. Part numbers, quantities and honest condition details help us assess the stock properly.

STEP 02

Commercial assessment

We assess condition, demand, likely resale value, selling timescale and the work required before the equipment can be offered to an end user.

STEP 03

Choose your route

We may propose an immediate purchase, a selective offer, managed resale, or a combination of routes for a mixed lot.

STEP 04

Collection and payment

For cash purchases, terms are agreed before collection. For managed resale, we agree the split and reporting process before taking stock.
