Important lifecycle distinction

The Series 90-30 is a discontinued PLC platform. RX3i is not universally obsolete: individual CPUs and modules have different lifecycle positions. GE Fanuc PLC hardware is distinct from FANUC CNC and servo equipment.

Successors and migration considerations

  • GE Fanuc Series 90-30 (IC693): PACSystems RX3i (IC695/IC694)
  • PACSystems RX3i CPU310 / CPU315 / CPU320: CPU310 > CPE310; CPU315 > CPE310 or CPE330; CPU320 > CPE330
  • PACSystems RX3i platform (IC695/IC694), incl. CPE302/305/310/330: n/a (current range)
  • VersaMax Micro (IC200U): Emerson positions PACSystems RSTi-EP / RX3i for new designs (not an official like-for-like)

Migration is an engineering assessment, not an automatic drop-in substitution. Check program conversion, field wiring, communications, safety and commissioning requirements.

Questions engineers ask

Does discontinued mean an installed controller must be replaced immediately?

No. It means the product's commercial lifecycle has changed, not that every installed unit has stopped functioning. Review spares availability, reliability, risk and your maintenance strategy.

Can Automation Outlet help source a legacy spare?

Yes. Send us the exact manufacturer part number, revision or series, quantity and required condition. Availability and testing status must be confirmed before an order.

Can a listed successor be swapped directly into my system?

Not necessarily. Replacement can require changes to software, wiring, communications, safety assessments and commissioning. Confirm compatibility with manufacturer documentation.
